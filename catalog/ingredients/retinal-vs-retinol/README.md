# Retinal vs. Retinol

Genuinely thinner than the other entries — flagged as a gap rather than
padded out. Unlike PDRN, snail mucin, or ginseng, there's no design spike
and no `videos/` project for this one in the Story Board repo yet.

**What exists:** a validated script titled *"Retinal vs Retinol: What the
Research Actually Compared"* — a comparison-format piece (retinal and
retinol are related but distinct actives; the video's job is explaining
what the research actually supports for each, not treating them as
synonyms). It's tracked here only as two generated QA artifacts at the repo
root — `storyline_retinal-vs-retinol.html` (structural/sourcing/content-safety
audit) and `storyboard_retinal-vs-retinol.html` (clip review board) — both
gitignored output, not source, and too large to usefully preview inline
here. The actual source script lives in the sibling SeoulHabit project, not
in this repo.

**If this ingredient gets picked up next:** there's no poster or component
to start from — it would begin from scratch, the way PDRN's poster or
ginseng's "two routes" project did, rather than reuse an existing spike.
