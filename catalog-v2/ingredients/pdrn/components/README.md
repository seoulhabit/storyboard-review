# Component: PDRN route comparison

Use this whenever a story moves between a cosmetic bottle, microneedling or another procedure, and medical injection evidence.

## Stable groups

- `#route-topical`
- `#route-assisted`
- `#route-injected`
- `#boundary`

These groups may be revealed independently. Keep each route's evidence text inside its own group.

## Motion recommendation

Reveal the route label first, then the study scope, then its status pill. The final boundary should land only after at least two routes are visible. Use restrained vertical movement and opacity; no needle impact, skin puncture, pulsing cells, or spinning helix.

## Rules

- Do not remove the word `specific` from the topical card.
- Do not use the injected card inside a topical-only frame unless the comparison itself is the message.
- Procedure-assisted stays evidence-empty until a traceable record is added to the pack.
- Source IDs must remain paired with the displayed route.
