# Skin macro photography

Real-looking (model-generated) macro skin/hand photography for tactile
proof beats — the class of image `catalog/product-photography/` and
`catalog/ingredient-photography/` don't cover: real skin texture rather
than product packaging or flat-lay ingredient shots.

## Why this exists

`catalog/product-photography/README.md`'s own note ("no faces, hands
cropped at the wrist") already anticipated skin/hand macros as a category,
but nothing had been generated in it before `videos/pilling-vs-peeling`'s
2026-09-01 recut needed real tactile evidence for a pilling-vs-peeling
differential (see that project's `BRIEF.md` § Round 2 § Imagery decision
record for the full Why/Precedent/Scope filing this harvest follows).

## What's here

| File | Subject |
|---|---|
| `01-pilling-macro.png` | Fingertip rolling dry skincare residue into small crumbs on the back of a hand/wrist |
| `02-layering-hand.png` | Three skincare textures (gel/lotion/sunscreen) freshly layered on the back of a hand |
| `03-flaking-skin.png` | Genuinely dry, flaking forearm skin — small translucent flakes lifting at the edges |
| `04-base-skin.png` | Clean, bare, smooth forearm/hand skin — no product, no flaking (a comparison base) |

All four: 1200×1200 PNG, one consistent grade applied
(`eq=saturation=0.62:contrast=1.06`, desaturated warm-grey/ivory tones),
generated via Higgsfield `nano_banana_pro`. No faces, hands/forearms
cropped tight (no elbow/shoulder), no product bottles or packaging in
frame, no text/logos/claims.

## Reuse notes

- `04-base-skin.png` is deliberately generic enough to serve as a "before"
  or neutral comparison base — reuse it rather than generating a new bare-
  skin plate when a project needs one and doesn't require a specific
  visible condition.
- These plates carry the *tactile/evidentiary* role in a composition —
  proving what a viewer should look for. Keep the *explanatory* role
  (mechanism diagrams) browser-drawn; don't let a generated plate stand in
  for a mechanism it wasn't built to show.
- Status: validated in one shipped render
  (`videos/pilling-vs-peeling/renders/pilling-vs-peeling.mp4`), not yet
  reused by a second project. Update this note once one does.
