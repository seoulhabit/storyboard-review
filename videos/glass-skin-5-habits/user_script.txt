Faceless Script: The 5-Habit K-Beauty Routine (30 Seconds)

Time | Visual | Audio (Voiceover) & Text Overlay
0:00-0:03 | Pattern Interrupt: Top-down shot of a bathroom counter covered in 10+ skincare bottles. Two hands enter the frame and firmly slide most of them out of view. | "Stop doing a 10-step skincare routine. You're just destroying your skin barrier."
0:03-0:07 | The Promise: Macro shot of a thick, glassy moisturizer being swiped across the back of a perfectly hydrated, glowing hand. | "Korean glass skin doesn't come from hoarding products. It comes from these 5 simple habits."
0:07-0:12 | Habits 1 & 2: Hands emulsifying a cleansing balm under running water, followed by a slow-motion pour of watery toner into a cupped palm. | "One: Cleanse. Melt the SPF off. Two: Hydrate. Press toner into damp skin."
0:12-0:18 | Habits 3 & 4: A glass dropper dispensing one drop of serum onto a clear glass dish, then hands smoothing a rich barrier cream. | "Three: Treat. Pick just one active serum. Four: Seal. Lock it all in with a barrier cream."
0:18-0:24 | Habit 5: Close-up of two fingers. Sunscreen is squeezed in two perfect lines down the length of the pointer and middle fingers. | "And Five: Protect. Two fingers of SPF 50, every single morning."
0:24-0:30 | The CTA: Clean, aesthetic line-up of just 5 products on an empty counter. A finger taps the counter next to them. | "That's it. 5 habits. Hit subscribe because tomorrow we're fixing your damaged skin barrier."

Faceless Visual & Production Rules
- Macro Textures: Without facial expressions to hold retention, your visuals must be highly tactile. Zoom in close on water droplets, thick creams, and glossy serums.
- Dynamic Captions: Keep text on screen at all times. Highlight the words CLEANSE, HYDRATE, TREAT, SEAL, and PROTECT in a bold, aesthetic font in the center-left of the screen.
- ASMR Audio: Layer crisp sound effects (water splashing, jars unscrewing, cream swooshing) gently underneath your voiceover to make the video feel immersive.
