# Negatives — fictional K-beauty product photography

Appended to every assembled prompt after the scene body. Keep this list
in sync with `style-core.md`'s exclusions — this is the enforcement side,
not a second source of new rules.

## Lighting / material artifacts

glow, bloom, lens flare, glossy reflection, wet-glass highlight, specular
highlight, rim light, chromatic aberration, subsurface scattering sparkle,
motion blur, depth-of-field bokeh blur, tilt-shift blur

## Identity / brand

real brand name, brand logo, wordmark, trademark symbol, recognizable trade
dress, copied packaging design, any actual company's product silhouette

## People

human face, visible face, body beyond a wrist crop, multiple hands unless
specified

## Claims

badge, seal, award stamp, certification mark, percentage sign, numeric
efficacy claim, clinical claim text, "clinically proven", "dermatologist
tested", "whitening", "anti-aging", "wrinkle", "firming", "brightening" as
label copy, any efficacy or benefit wording anywhere in frame

## Typography

malformed Hangul, gibberish glyphs, mixed extra languages beyond Korean +
Latin volume unit, watermark, stock-photo watermark, text artifact, blurry
text, illegible label

## Color / palette violations

warm gradient background, earth-tone palette, neon color, saturated rainbow
colors, more than one accent color, metallic gold or rose-gold trim

## Scene clutter

cluttered or busy background, props unrelated to skincare (food, jewelry,
flowers, greenery as filler), extra unlisted objects, low resolution, jpeg
artifact
