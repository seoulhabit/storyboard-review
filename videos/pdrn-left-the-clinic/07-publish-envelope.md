## Title

The Viral Ingredient That Left the Korean Clinic (PDRN, Explained)

## Description

PDRN — DNA fragments first known from Korean skin-booster injections — is
now in ordinary serums, creams and masks. This is what actually changed
when it moved from a needle to a bottle: where it comes from, why "vegan
PDRN" exists, the one real topical trial, and 106 Korean regulatory
violations for ads that made it sound like medicine.

Sources: `content/ingredients/pdrn.json` and `content/findings/pdrn.json`,
seoulhabit-learn @ `cacff81`. MFDS violation data via Korean National
Assembly Health & Welfare Committee disclosure (Rep. Seo Young-seok,
2026), cross-checked across independent trade-press reports. Full sourcing
table: `01-story-brief.md` §Sourcing in this project.

This is not medical advice. It's a evidence check on what's actually been
tested, not a recommendation to use or avoid PDRN.
