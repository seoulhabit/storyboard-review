# Collagen ingredient pack

Status: **draft visual-ready**  
Version: **0.1.0**  
Primary harvest source: `videos/collagen-where-did-it-go`

This pack gives collagen a reusable visual identity while preserving the
distinction between a structural protein, a topical formula, an oral
collagen-peptide format, and the fragments present after digestion.

## Included

- `components/ingredient-actor.svg` — transparent dermal-fibre actor.
- `components/ingredient-actor.json` — stable layers, motion contract and safe-use rules.
- `components/identity-format-map.svg` — four-state identity and route map.
- `identity-card.svg` — compact ingredient identity card.
- `pack.json` — governed facts, evidence boundaries and story inputs.
- `manifest.json` — file-level status and provenance.

## Visual rule

The actor is a collagen recognition cue, not microscopy, proof of penetration,
proof of supplementation efficacy, or a depiction of a specific commercial
material. A consuming story must supply the exact form, route and evidence.

## Current limits

- No photographic collagen source or product media has been approved.
- No shared neutral cream/powder vessel is owned by this ingredient pack.
- The evidence records are traceable, but the complete pack remains draft
  until a scientific review and real-label stress test are completed.
