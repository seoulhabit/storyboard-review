AM vs PM
Skincare

What actually changes between sunrise and sundown — and why the order you apply things in still matters.

Morning Routine

Morning = Protect

  1. 1

    Cleanser

    Remove sweat & oil from overnight

  2. 2

    Toner

    Refresh & prep the skin

  3. 3

    Antioxidant Serum

    Brighten & fight free radicals

  4. 4

    Moisturizer

    Hydrate & soften skin

  5. 5

    Sunscreen

    Protect from UV rays

Night Routine

Night = Treat + Replenish

  1. 1

    Cleanser

    Remove makeup & buildup

  2. 2

    Toner

    Rebalance after cleansing

  3. 3

    Treatment

    Target acne, aging, or pigmentation

  4. 4

    Serum or Booster

    Deep hydration & nourishment

  5. 5

    Night Cream

    Lock in moisture overnight
