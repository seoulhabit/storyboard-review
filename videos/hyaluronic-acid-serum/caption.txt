You've heard hyaluronic acid holds 1,000x its weight in water 💧 — that's actually a myth. Here's what it really does (and the one mistake that cancels it out).

The real story:
🧪 Your body makes HA naturally — production drops ~6%/decade
🧽 Think "sponge," not "magic" — it plumps skin cells by holding water
💦 The golden rule: apply to DAMP skin, not dry — dry skin + HA can pull moisture OUT instead of in
🔒 Always seal with a moisturizer after
