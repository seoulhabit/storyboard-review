## Three alternative 3-second hooks

an absolute.

1. **(shipped)** "A 1999 study on radiation-damaged skin sparked today's skincare craze. TikTok skipped the study part." — leads with the real citation, sets up the whole video's method (checking what's actually behind a trend).
2. "Every snail-mucin bottle claims the same secret ingredient. The lab results say otherwise." — leads with the variability finding (C6), pulls the payoff earlier but reveals less of the story's arc.
3. "One snail extract has real clinical trials behind it. The one in your bathroom probably doesn't." — leads with the sourced/unsourced split (C2/C3) directly, the most confrontational option, best if the goal is maximum pattern-interrupt at the cost of narrative smoothness.

Hook 1 selected: it is the only one of the three that states a real historical fact rather than an
inference, and "TikTok changed the science" — this video's whole thesis — needs the origin story in
frame 0 to land.
