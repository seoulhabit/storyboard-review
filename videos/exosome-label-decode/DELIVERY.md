## Captions

- **Burned in** — 27 beat-timed cues ship *inside* the render, in a reserved
  lane at y 1360–1520. Nothing to attach separately. The end card
  (55.800–59.000) is deliberately silent and carries no cue.
- **Sidecar** — `captions/exosome-label-decode.srt` and
  `captions/exosome-label-decode.vtt` (paste either into the upload flow).

Both outputs come from one table in `scripts/build-captions.py`, so they
cannot drift apart. Cue timing is derived from **measured** phrase boundaries
in the final mixed VO (`ffmpeg silencedetect` @ −38dB), not estimated; the
words are the authored script, so proper nouns are correct by construction.
Enforced at build time: ≥1.0s per cue (shortest shipped: 1.030s), max 2 lines,
max 6 words per line.

> **Scope guard, worth keeping in the description.** All three reviews cover

## End screen / next video

**`07-endcard` (55.800–59.000)** is the channel end card: the **습 mark**
(construction taken from `brand/channel/avatar-800.html` — Noto Sans KR 500,
`--ink`, optically raised because Hangul syllable blocks sit high in the em
box), the SEOULHABIT wordmark, the coral rule, "Korean skincare, answered.",
and the one specific CTA. The mark also now appears in `06-close`'s sign-off
lockup.

Shorts carry no end-screen overlay, so the end card is built to **preserve the
loop rather than dead-end it**: same paper ground, same two-column grid, plate
in the same right column at the same scale (1.0) that `01-hook` opens from. Its
brand block was trimmed 73px after measurement, because the untrimmed version
sat the bottle 75px lower than `01-hook`'s; a 50% overlay of the first and last
frames now superimposes the cap, body and label cleanly. The mix is live to the
final frame, so a replay inherits no dead beat.

Next-video handoff is the CTA itself: viewers comment a product, and that
becomes the next label-decode.
