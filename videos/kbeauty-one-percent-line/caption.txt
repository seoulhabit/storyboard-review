The 1% K-Beauty Secret Brands Are Hiding
Hook (on-screen): 80% Ginseng? What if it's actually less than a single drop.

The cheat sheet:
1. The Extract Loophole — "70% Centella Extract" usually means a tiny bit of plant steeped in mostly water.
2. The 1% Line — ingredients are listed by concentration only until the 1% mark; after that, order means nothing.
3. Spot the line — preservatives like Phenoxyethanol (hard-capped ~1% in the EU) or Ethylhexylglycerin (same fractions-of-a-percent range) mark where "under 1%" starts.
4. Live teardown: Beauty of Joseon Glow Serum — Propolis Extract leads, Niacinamide sits at 2%, everything after Ethylhexylglycerin (dextrin, xanthan gum, tocopherol) is trace amounts.
5. Hanbang cheat sheet — Snail Secretion Filtrate = Snail Mucin (barrier repair). Panax Root = Ginseng (firming). Artemisia Princeps = Mugwort (calming).
