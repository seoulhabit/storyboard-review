That viral exosome serum?
The word “exosome”
tells you almost nothing.
Exosomes are microscopic particles
cells release to carry signals.
But in skincare, they can come
from very different sources.
And those sources are not
automatically interchangeable.
The research is promising,
but human evidence
for topical exosome skincare
is still limited.
Many studies also combine exosomes
with procedures like microneedling,
making it difficult to credit
the serum alone.
So before you buy one,
ask three questions.
What is the source?
Has the ingredient been
properly characterized,
and kept stable?
And was this exact
finished product
tested on people?
If the brand only says
“exosome technology,”
that is a marketing claim.
Not proof of a miracle.
Promising? Yes.
Proven miracle?
Not yet.
