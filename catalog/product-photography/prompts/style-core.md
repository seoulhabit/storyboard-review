# Style core — fictional K-beauty product photography

Shared look for every scene in this set. Prepended to every scene body at
generation time. Change the look here, once — never inside a scene body.

Final prompt assembly = `style-core.md` + `scenes.json[n].body` + `negatives.md`.

## What this is

Unbranded studio product photography of **fictional** Korean skincare
packaging, built to 2026 K-beauty conventions: skinimalism, mono-material
construction (glass, aluminium, or uncoated paperboard — never mixed flashy
materials), refill systems, transparent walls that reveal product texture,
hinged pad jars with integrated tweezers, second-generation airless cushions.
No real product, brand, or company is depicted or implied.

## Material and lighting

- Matte, unlit, flat-shaded rendering. No specular highlights, no glossy
  wet-glass reflections, no bloom, no glow, no lens flare.
- High-key, even studio lighting. Soft mist-grey contact shadows only —
  never dramatic or directional shadow play.
- Explicitly avoid the glossy wet-glass K-beauty default look.

## Palette

- **Paper white** — background and primary surfaces.
- **Mist grey** — secondary surfaces, shadows, secondary packaging.
- **Celadon** — primary packaging material color.
- **Ink** — fine linework and typography.
- **One muted, desaturated aqua accent maximum per frame** — never more than
  one aqua element, never saturated or neon, applied to a single small detail
  (a cap ring, a dispenser button, a liquid tint) and nowhere else in that
  frame.

No other colors. No warm gradients, no earth tones, no metallic gold or
rose-gold trim, no rainbow or multi-accent palettes.

## Construction and composition

- Mono-material builds per object: frosted or clear glass, brushed or matte
  aluminium, or uncoated paperboard. Never mix flashy materials on one piece.
- Skinimalism: minimal ornament, generous negative space, no faux-luxury
  detailing.
- Where the scene calls for it: visible refill mechanisms, transparent walls
  showing product texture through the wall, hinged lids, integrated
  tweezers, airless-pump internals.
- Clean seamless studio backdrop, centered or rule-of-thirds framing
  appropriate to the aspect ratio.

## Labeling

- If a scene specifies a label, render it small, minimal, and legible: plain
  sans-serif Hangul, ink-colored, reading **only** the given Korean category
  word plus its volume/count (e.g. `세럼 · 30mL`). Nothing else on the label —
  no additional words, no logo mark, no symbol, no seal.
- If a scene specifies no label, the object carries no visible text at all.

## Hard exclusions (identity and claims)

- No real brand names, logos, wordmarks, trademarks, or recognizable trade
  dress from any actual company or product line.
- No human faces. A hand may appear only where a scene explicitly calls for
  one, cropped at the wrist, face never in frame.
- No percentages, no durations, no efficacy or benefit language, no badges,
  no seals, no award stamps, anywhere in the frame — labels carry nominal
  fields only.
