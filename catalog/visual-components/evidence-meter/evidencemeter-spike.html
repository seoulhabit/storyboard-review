EvidenceMeter

A precision readout, not a loading bar. Hero claim, then the instrument, then footnotes on one shared baseline — flat, clinical, physically bound to its claim or route, and driven by a deterministic renderFrame(t) clock.

No volume

Flat fill only. Depth on a readout implies magnitude — an exaggerated claim.

No success color

Celadon/aqua at every fill level. 2-of-4 must not read as failing, 4-of-4 must not read as a win.

Locked to claim

Never renders alone — always paired with its claim (or route) and source chip.

Zero margin drift

Fixed 380px box, reserved claim height. Typography never shifts the container.
