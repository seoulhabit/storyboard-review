# PDRN — production card

## Use these repeatedly

- Recognition actor: short DNA fragments, not a giant spinning helix.
- Product actor: restrained ivory dropper on cream.
- Truth mechanic: “route first.”
- Evidence split: injected medical evidence versus defined topical-formula evidence.
- Boundary: source, fragment size, concentration, vehicle, and delivery route can change what a result means.

## Best one-sentence position

PDRN has credible but route-specific science; the label on a cosmetic bottle does not automatically inherit the evidence behind an injected medical preparation.

## Three ready story angles

1. **Why route matters:** injection evidence is not serum evidence.
2. **The topical evidence update:** one small 2026 split-face trial is promising for a specific PDRN-850K eye cream.
3. **How to read a PDRN label:** front-label PDRN does not reveal source, fragment profile, concentration, or equivalence.

## Minimum visual recipe for any format

1. Bottle-versus-clinic hook.
2. Identity card.
3. Route comparison.
4. One human-study card with the exact preparation.
5. Boundary card.
