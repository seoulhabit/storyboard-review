## SEO rationale

Keyword `korean skincare routine` is the only head term in this niche that grew
demand over the last 30 days (+17.5%), while `korean skincare` (−12.7%),
`skincare routine` (−24.8%), `glass skin` (−14.9%) and `korean glass skin`
(−34.0%) all contracted (VidIQ, US market, captured 2026-08-30). The opening
sentence leads with the growing term, not the larger-but-shrinking ones.

`darker skin tones` targets `korean skincare for dark skin` (3,347 est.
monthly searches, competition 25.3/100) — the lowest-competition demographic
qualifier in the keyword set, and the strongest single outlier in a 3-month
VidIQ breakout scan was a glass-skin video aimed explicitly at Black men
(151K-sub channel, 1.64x its own baseline).

Named ingredients (snail mucin, centella, retinal, PDRN, red ginseng) match
the topics already produced in this repo's video library, so the description
describes the real catalog rather than an aspirational one.
