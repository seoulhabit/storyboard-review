## 2. The two operator instructions, executed

### R6 — cut

"Madecassoside / DESCRIBED AS PRIMARY ACTIVE" is gone from the cut, from the
voiceover, and from the beat sheet.

Cut rather than corrected, deliberately. No source ranks madecassoside first, and
**both** efficacy-bearing sources name a different compound: S2 says the wound
healing properties "appear particularly effective when the extract contains
greater amounts of AS [asiaticoside], being one of its more active compounds",
and S3 names **asiatic acid** as "one of the active constituents". Rewriting the
line to name asiaticoside or asiatic acid would have been authoring a *new*
claim under cover of a sourcing pass, so the row was removed instead.

`R5` was re-ordered on screen to the source's own order — asiatic acid,
asiaticoside, madecassoside, madecassic acid — so nothing on screen implies a
ranking any more.

### R7 — sourced, by re-scoping

The row had three limbs; only the first had support, and only in wound healing.

| Limb | Verdict |
|---|---|
| "quiet inflammation signals" | **Sourced** to S2 |
| "fibroblasts switch back on" | **Removed.** S2's only fibroblast sentence is *proliferation* after asiaticoside, not dormant→reactivation; S3's has asiatic acid **inhibiting** collagen in keloid fibroblasts |
| "rebuild the barrier" | **Removed.** `barrier` is 0 hits in S3; S2 is a wound-healing review |

Supporting sentence, verbatim: *"[Centella asiatica] has shown an
anti-inflammatory effect observed by the reduction in Interleukin-1β (IL-1β),
Interleukin-6 (IL-6) and Tumour Necrosis Factor α (TNFα) [,,], as well as
prostaglandin E2 (PGE2) [,], and cyclooxygenase-2 (COX-2) []."*

The two unsupported limbs were **removed, not softened**. The scope the source
does carry is on screen — the scene ends on **"IN WOUND HEALING"**, and the next
scene says the review is not about daily skincare.

### R8 — rewritten, because the cut made it false

"No source record exists in this system" stopped being true the moment R7 gained
a citation. It now reads: *"That review is about wound healing, not daily
skincare. The routine advice below has no source record here."* Leaving the old
line would have shipped a factual error on screen.

## 5. What is still true after this pass

- **The citation is not about barriers.** S2 is a wound-healing review; two of
  its four trials were oral. The video, the description and the pinned comment
  all say so. `K-2b` clearing is a statement about the *ratio*, not a statement
  that the barrier story is now evidenced.
- **R3 and R9 are still unsourced.** The symptom framing and the routine advice
  have no support in any of the three sources, and both still carry flags.
- **S1 was fetched and used for nothing.** Recorded as resolved and off-topic
  rather than pressed into service.
- **The source that would actually change this video** is a topical-centella
  trial with a **barrier endpoint** — TEWL or stratum-corneum hydration — in
  intact skin. Not another wound scale.
