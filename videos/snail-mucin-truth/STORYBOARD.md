## Video direction

- **palette system** (from `frame.md`): `green`/`green-deep` (ink_moss `#2c4a34` /
  ink_dark `#0f2016`) = gravity ground, reserved for Frame 1 (hook) and Frame 6
  (outro) — the video's two callback bookends. `cream`/`cream-2` (pearl
  `#fbfaf4` / pale sage `#e8efdd`) = the body/reading ground for Frames 2–5.
  `pink`/`pink-deep` (shell `#b9835a` / shell_deep `#8a5a37`) = the one warm
  accent — CTA chips, spring-pop payoffs, step badges. The motif-only
  seafoam/gold/lilac trail sequence (`#bdf0d4`/`#f2e3b4`/`#e4c8ec`) is
  reserved to Frame 1's goo-drip glyph and its faint Frame 6 reprise —
  nowhere else (`frame.md` → Snail Mucin Brand Notes).
- **type**: display + body = Bricolage Grotesque (weight 500 display / 400
  body, opsz engaged); chrome = JetBrains Mono 500 uppercase for labels, step
  badges, and every `CITATION ⌞ ING-snail-mucin-S0NN ⌟` chip.
- **motion grammar**: `power3` long-tail settle by default; no
  `back.out`/`bounce.out` except the hook's one restrained, capped spring-pop
  (matching the locked poster's own capped `back.out(1.4)` — never the
  literal 2.2). Reveals sequence in the back ~50% of each frame, timed to the
  VO's spoken cues — never front-loaded. Internal seams (Frame 5's
  step-to-step swap) are velocity-matched cuts, not hard slideshow cuts.
- **rhythm / held-frame allocation**: Frame 3 (what-is) and Frame 6 (outro)
  are this video's two deliberate held/breather beats — content resolves
  early in each and holds still (subtle jitter only) rather than continuing
  to animate. Frames 1, 2, 4, and 5 carry the active reveal motion.
- **faceless visuals — no photoreal media.** Every visual element (the
  goo-drip glyph, the snail-on-leaf illustration, the droplet/pat/seal step
  glyphs) is an **invented vector graphic** in this palette, re-authored in
  the locked poster's own hand-drawn-SVG style — not sourced or
  AI-generated photography. The **one exception** is Frame 6's bottle shot
  (`catalog/ingredient-photography/12-snail-mucin.png`), a real catalog
  asset reframed into this project's `assets/`, not AI media.
- **negative list (video-wide — extends the list below)**: no lazy
  breathing, no bad slow pan/push in any frame's back half; no dumping a
  frame's content in its first ~25%; no default-AI purple-blue
  gradient/bokeh cliché; no real interface mockups (not this topic).

## Video negative list

- No absolute/superlative language: incredible, instantly, must, proven, cures,
  erases, guaranteed, miracle.
- No unattributed cruelty-free assertion — always "producers say," never a
  bare fact.
- "Acne scars" is never spoken or shown — "post-acne marks" only (the
  evidence is for hyperpigmentation, not true scarring).
- No brand mark, logo, or URL lockup anywhere, including the outro (inherited
  from the locked poster / `../snail-mucin-glass-skin/shot-plan.json`
  `no_brand_mark`).
- No color/id beyond the four locked source ids below is ever invented or
  displayed as a citation.
- The slime-trail seafoam/gold/lilac motif (Frame 1, reprised faintly in
  Frame 6) is decorative only — never used as a citation or confidence color
  (see `frame.md` → Snail Mucin Brand Notes → success-color law).
