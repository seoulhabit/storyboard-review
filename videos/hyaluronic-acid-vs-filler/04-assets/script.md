## HOOK — s01-lineup · chapter "Three things, one name"

**KIMBERLY:** Your hyaluronic-acid serum cannot do what filler does. Same name — three completely different jobs. The kind your body makes. The kind in your serum. The kind a doctor injects. <!-- C1 nominal + C0 sourced (thesis) -->

## MISCONCEPTION — s02-not-filler, s03-origin · chapters "Not filler in a bottle", "It came out of a cow's eye"

**KIMBERLY:** And the mix-up is expensive. People buy a serum expecting what an injection does. A serum is not filler in a bottle. <!-- [GEN] states the video's central question as a claim -->

**KIMBERLY:** The name goes back to nineteen thirty-four, when it was isolated from the clear jelly inside a cow's eye. <!-- C2 · chip J Biol Chem · 1934 · compressed from two scenes to one sentence, cow-eye reference preserved for the closing callback -->

## MECHANISM — s04-body, s05-compare, s06-serum-size, s07-plumping

**KIMBERLY:** Your body already makes its own. Most of it sits in your skin, holding water. It also lubricates your joints, and it's still doing its job inside your eye. <!-- C3 · chip Dermatoendocrinol · 2012 — C4 · chip Front Vet Sci · 2019 -->

**KIMBERLY:** Here's where the two versions really split. A serum's version stays mostly at the surface, supporting hydration. A filler's version is completely different chemistry — cross-linked into a gel and placed beneath the skin, where it can physically add volume. <!-- C5 (serum half) + C8/C9 (filler half) — the side-by-side comparison, landed before 1:00 per the revision brief -->

**KIMBERLY:** In a serum, size decides almost everything. Large chains mostly stay near the surface, forming a hydrating film. Smaller ones may travel farther into the upper layers of skin. Neither one behaves like an injectable filler. <!-- C5 · chip Skin Res Technol · 2015 · note the hedge "may" -->

**KIMBERLY:** So why does the bottle say plumping? Because hydrated surface cells can temporarily make fine lines appear softer — like watering a tired houseplant. Not like adding a new branch. <!-- C6 · "temporarily" + "appear" carry into the on-screen type, [K-3] -->

## PROOF — s08-binds-water, s09-lifeguard, s10-crosslink

**KIMBERLY:** And here's the part the marketing skips. Hyaluronic acid binds water and holds onto it. It does not manufacture water out of nothing. A complete formula still needs something that stops that water leaving. <!-- C7 · chip ChemRxiv · 2023 -->

**KIMBERLY:** Skip that step, and you're hiring a lifeguard for an empty swimming pool. Strange image. Correct principle. <!-- editorial, C12 -->

**KIMBERLY:** A filler is chemically different. Its chains are cross-linked into a stable, connected grid that holds its own shape, once it's placed beneath the skin. <!-- C8 · chip J Cosmet Dermatol · 2024 — mechanism of the transform shown in s10, distinct from the s05 preview -->

## APPLICATION — s11-do-not-inject · chapter "Do not inject yourself"

**KIMBERLY:** Is a filler just serum with a needle? Not even close. That's worth putting in very large letters. <!-- rhetorical aside, Jay's line converted -->

> **ON-SCREEN, full frame:** DO NOT INJECT YOURSELF. <!-- C10 · REWORDED from "Never inject a topical serum" to track FDA's own sentence · chip FDA · Dermal Fillers -->

**KIMBERLY:** Do not inject yourself. That's the F D A's own wording, not ours. Dermal fillers are a medical procedure with real risks, including tissue death, vision loss and stroke. Choose a provider trained to perform the injection. <!-- C10, C11 · chip FDA · Dermal Fillers -->

## RECAP + END SCENE — s12-badges, s13-endcard · chapter "Same family, different jobs"

**KIMBERLY:** Same ingredient family. Different size, different structure, different location — so, a different result. The body's version, a resident. The serum's version, a moisturiser. The filler's version, a construction project. <!-- C12 editorial — the three visual identities -->

**KIMBERLY:** Serum hydrates. Filler adds volume. Same name, different jobs. And yes — we told you the cow's eye would come back. <!-- final message, per the revision brief revealed early enough to hold ≥3s; cow-eye closing callback -->

> **END CARD:** Serum hydrates. Filler adds volume. Same name, different jobs.
