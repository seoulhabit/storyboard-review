01-hook (0:00.10-0:04.92)

Collagen cream does not replace your collagen. Collagen powder does not travel straight to your face.

So where does it go? Across twenty three trials, it appears to help. Remove the industry funded studies, and the answer changes.

03-building (0:14.03-0:20.87)

Think of your skin as a building. Collagen is the main structure inside it. It keeps everything firm and upright.

  • C1: Definitional -- collagen is a main structural protein of skin.

04-demolition (0:21.17-0:30.40)

As we age, collagen production slows. And ultraviolet light switches on enzymes that cut collagen apart. The sun runs a demolition crew. It works weekends.

On-screen citation: J Invest Dermatol · 1998

  • C2: Fisher & Voorhees, J Investig Dermatol Symp Proc 1998;3(1):61-8 (PMID 9732061); companion Fisher et al., Photochem Photobiol 1999;69(2):154-7.

05-boundary (0:30.70-0:38.95)

Sunscreen is not just about sunburn. It draws a boundary around the building. Preserving collagen is usually easier than replacing it later.

06-door (0:39.30-0:50.50)

Now, the cream. To pass through skin, a molecule generally needs to be under five hundred daltons. Collagen is around three hundred thousand. It is not getting through that door.

On-screen citation: Exp Dermatol · 2000

  • C3: Bos & Meinardi, Exp Dermatol 2000;9(3):165-9 -- the "500 dalton rule" for what can cross the skin barrier.

Visual-only information: On screen: two size cards are compared directly -- "~500 daltons, the size limit" and "~300,000 daltons, one collagen molecule" -- so the ~600x size gap is a visual comparison as much as a spoken one.

07-film (0:50.80-0:58.79)

It may still form a moisturising film on the surface. Skin may feel smoother. But polishing the windows is not replacing the beams.

  • C4: Follows from C3 (too large to cross the barrier); no efficacy claim made.

In twenty twenty five, researchers pooled twenty three randomised trials. All together, a benefit. Keep only the studies without industry funding, and it is no longer statistically significant. Keep only the higher quality studies. Same result. The effect stops showing up.

14-hierarchy (1:57.94-2:11.25)

What the evidence actually supports. Broad spectrum sunscreen, every day. Not smoking. Enough protein and vitamin C. And for suitable users, topical retinoids have considerably stronger evidence for encouraging collagen production than collagen cream.
