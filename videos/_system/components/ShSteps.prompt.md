Ordered sequence for application and routine order.

```jsx
<ShSteps steps={[{n:1,action:'Cleanse',note:'Lukewarm water only.'},{n:2,action:'Apply damp'},{n:3,action:'Seal'}]} />
```

Three or four steps. Clay numerals at 0.6 × display, a brass connector between them, an optional muted one-line note per step.
