5 Habits, Not 10 Steps: The Real Korean Glass Skin Routine

2. Hydrate — press toner into damp skin.
3. Treat — pick just one active serum.
4. Seal — lock it in with a barrier cream.
5. Protect — two fingers of SPF 50, every morning.
