# Snail Mucin — production card

## Use these repeatedly

- Recognition actor: clear elastic gel strand.
- Product actor: ivory airless pump cutout.
- Source actor: real-looking snail macro, used only to identify animal origin.
- Truth mechanic: “specific extract versus the whole category.”
- Strongest teaching visual: extraction method changes measured composition.

## Best one-sentence position

Snail mucin is an interesting variable filtrate with a few small formula-specific studies—not a standardized miracle ingredient.

## Three ready story angles

1. **What the label actually means:** Snail Mucin on the front; Snail Secretion Filtrate in the ingredient list.
2. **Why two snail products may differ:** extraction method, measured composition, and finished formula.
3. **What the studies really tested:** one named extract or combination regimen, not every bottle on the shelf.

## Minimum visual recipe for any format

1. Tactile hook.
2. Identity card.
3. Source or process image.
4. One evidence card.
5. Boundary card.

Do not show every available asset in one video. Select two or three recurring assets and let the format provide the rest of the novelty.
