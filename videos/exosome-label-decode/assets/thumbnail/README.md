**Shipped:** `final.png` (a copy of `graded-a-hook-payoff.png`).

Extract-grade-finalize path: pulled from the finished render, not separately
authored — the composition already puts the hero object and a legible headline
in one frame, so a second asset was not needed.

| Candidate | Frame | Why it was tested |
|---|---|---|
| `a-hook-payoff` | t=1.95s | The hook's payoff — "EXOSOME?" + the coral claim bar + the bottle. **Chosen.** |
| `b-limited-stamp` | t=20.95s | The strongest single claim ("Human evidence / LIMITED"). |
| `c-claim-vs-proof` | t=46.10s | The thesis beat (claim ≠ proof). |

**Why A.** Judged at 135×240 (Shorts-shelf scale), not at full size. A is the
only candidate whose primary word is legible at a glance; B's headline is
already marginal there and C is the most text-dense and reads as a grey block.
A also carries the product, which is what the title is about.

**Caption-free by construction.** Every candidate was extracted inside a gap in
the caption schedule (A: 1.72–2.07s, B: 20.70–21.30s, C: 45.90–46.39s) and each
was verified to have zero ink in the caption band before grading — a burned-in
caption in a thumbnail is a shipped mistake.

**Grade.** Re-derived for this video's own palette, deliberately light:
contrast ×1.06, saturation ×1.10, unsharp (r2, 45%, t3). No vignette — this
palette is flat warm ivory by design, and a prior video's vignette-and-curve
chain reads as a muddy cast on it. Measured effect: mean luma 227.4 → 226.2,
mean chroma 10.20 → 11.77 (enough to hold the coral accent at grid size,
not enough to shift the ivory).
