Stop! Don't Ruin Your Skin Barrier (How to Patch Test Retinol) 🧴

New retinol in hand? Don't slather it on your whole face yet — patch test it first.

Your 48-hour patch test, step by step:
1. Pick a test site — behind your ear or along your jawline (skin that behaves most like your face)
2. At night, apply a pea-sized amount to clean, dry skin. Rub in gently. Nothing else on top.
3. Wait 48 hours. A little dryness is normal — but severe redness, stinging, or raised bumps means wash it off and reach for a gentler formula.

If your skin looks normal after 48 hours, you're ready to start using it slowly.

General patch-testing guide — not sourced claims, not medical advice.
