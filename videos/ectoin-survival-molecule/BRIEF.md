share a 1080 px short edge and type size is a fraction of the short edge, so

## Architecture — flat, one level

Root → frames only. **No act/chapter nesting.** `videos/pilling-vs-peeling/index.html`
records an isolated test where root→body→frame double-nesting silently broke a scene's
foot-zone layout, pushing content off-canvas — invisible in the rendered MP4, not a
lint finding. Same file: nested `<audio data-start>` is unverified against the pinned
CLI, so **all audio lives on the root**.

Consequence: ~40 flat scene clips plus ~40 VO clips, BGM and SFX on one root. That root
is generated by `scripts/build-index.py`, not hand-maintained.

CLI pin **0.8.22** (newest; `doctor` reports it as latest).

## The four script corrections

Applied to `SCRIPT.md`; each changes VO **and** on-screen copy.

1. **C7, the 104-women result.** The script said researchers "reported advantages in
   moisturization and measurements associated with skin appearance." Heinrich 2007
   reports a **subject preference** for the ectoin arm on a Wilcoxon rank-sum test —
   the women preferred it. That is not the same as an instrument measuring their skin,
   and stating it as such would be exactly the over-read this channel exists to avoid.
2. **C11, "most brands do not disclose their ectoin percentage."** Falsified —
   Paula's Choice and The Ordinary both put it in the product name. Replaced with the
   sharper, checkable claim: the big front-label number often is not the ectoin number.
3. **C3, preferential exclusion.** Yu 2007 actually found preferential *binding* as the
   overall tendency, with exclusion depending on how structured the protein's own
   hydration layer already is. The celebrity/security analogy stays — it is a good
   analogy — but it is now marked as the tidy version of a messier result.
4. **C8, "atopic dermatitis"** is glossed as eczema at first mention. The original
   used the clinical term first and the plain word only later, which inverts the
   plain-language rule.
