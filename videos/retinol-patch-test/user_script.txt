Title Idea: Stop! Don't Ruin Your Skin Barrier (How to Patch Test Retinol)
Vibe: Cautious but encouraging, educational, and aesthetic.

0:00 - 0:05
Visual: Hook — you holding the retinol serum looking excited, then putting up a hand to signal "stop."
VO: "So you just got a brand new Retinol and you want to slather it all over your face immediately? Stop!"

0:05 - 0:15
Visual: Context — close up of the product texture on your finger. Text on screen: Retinol = Powerful.
VO: "Retinol is the gold standard for anti-aging and breakouts, but it is incredibly strong. Before you risk a damaged skin barrier, you need to patch test. Here's how to do it safely."

0:15 - 0:25
Visual: Step 1 — zoom in on you pointing to the safe zone right behind the earlobe or along the jawline.
VO: "Because retinol speeds up cell turnover, test it right behind your ear or along your jawline. This mimics the skin on your face perfectly."

0:25 - 0:35
Visual: Step 2 — applying a tiny, pea-sized amount to the skin at night.
VO: "At night, apply a tiny, pea-sized amount to clean, dry skin. Rub it in gently, and then leave it alone. No other serums or moisturizers on top."

0:35 - 0:45
Visual: Step 3 — checking the spot. Text on screen: Wait 48 Hours.
VO: "Retinol reactions can be delayed, so wait 48 hours. A little dryness is normal, but if you see severe redness, stinging, or raised bumps, wash it off—your skin needs a gentler formula."

0:45 - 0:55
Visual: Outro — smiling, pointing down or to the side. Text on screen: seoulhabit.com.
VO: "If your skin looks normal, you're ready to start using it slowly. Check out the description or head to seoulhabit.com for our full guide on layering retinol!"
