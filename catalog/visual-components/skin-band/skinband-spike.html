SkinBand

A labelled two-layer skin cross-section (epidermis over dermis, a gently wavy surface line) that other actors compose within or beneath. Built for videos/hyaluronic-acid-vs-filler v2 -- a confirmed catalog gap at the time (three uncatalogued one-off cross-sections already existed elsewhere in the repo, none harvested) -- and used across four scenes to carry where each molecular form actually sits.

Natural HA · in the dermis
Serum · at the surface
Filler · beneath the skin
API. skin_band(w, h, boundary_frac=0.30) returns the wavy surface line, the dashed epidermis/dermis boundary at h * boundary_frac, and both layer labels as one SVG fragment -- compose it inside any viewBox, with other actors (free coils, a lattice, water dots) drawn above or below the boundary line as the claim requires. Labels are plain <text> at 22px / 55% opacity, never the citation-chip typography.

Two things measured, not assumed:
  1. Compose the lattice BEFORE the band, not after, when both share a viewBox. The band's own text labels must paint on top so they stay legible over a dense grid -- confirmed by a real bug during authoring: calling the band twice (once inside a shifted group, once again unshifted) produced two overlapping "EPIDERMIS" labels at different y-offsets, caught on an extracted frame, invisible to hyperframes check (which has no notion of "the same label drawn twice").
  2. The boundary line is a design fact, not a decoration -- boundary_frac should match what the beat is actually claiming (a serum panel wants the boundary high, near the surface; a filler panel wants structures shown reaching well beneath it). Getting this wrong makes an accurate label sit over the wrong geometry.
